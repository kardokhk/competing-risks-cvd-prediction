Chi-square test of independence between missingness and the three-level event indicator: chi-square = 330.6 on 2 df, *P* < 0.001.

- Crude risk difference for observed CVD death, incomplete minus complete: +2.84 percentage points (95% CI +2.17 to +3.52).
- Crude risk difference for observed competing death, incomplete minus complete: +6.25 percentage points (95% CI +5.27 to +7.24).

Crude proportions confound follow-up length, so the comparison that matters is the competing-risk cumulative incidence at a fixed horizon.

## 4. The quantity the complete-case analysis gets wrong

Aalen-Johansen cumulative incidence, unweighted:

| horizon | set | n | CVD CIF % (95% CI) | competing-death CIF % |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 y | full cohort | 41,151 | 1.586 (1.468 to 1.714) | 3.903 |
| 5 y | complete cases | 35,314 | 1.309 (1.197 to 1.437) | 3.276 |
| 5 y | incomplete cases | 5,837 | 3.252 (2.813 to 3.717) | 7.667 |
| 10 y | full cohort | 41,151 | 3.678 (3.477 to 3.882) | 9.300 |
| 10 y | complete cases | 35,314 | 3.299 (3.102 to 3.512) | 8.364 |
| 10 y | incomplete cases | 5,837 | 5.927 (5.207 to 6.491) | 14.822 |

At 10 y the complete-case CVD cumulative incidence is 3.299% against 3.678% in the full cohort, a relative shortfall of 10.3%.

This is the number that makes complete-case analysis untenable as the primary analysis. Every model in the benchmark is calibrated against an observed cumulative incidence, and E/O is a ratio whose denominator is exactly this quantity. A denominator that is low by this much moves every E/O, and it moves the naive and competing-risk arms by different amounts because the competing-death incidence is also displaced.

| 2017-2018 | 98.1% | 98.1% | 98.1% |

Censoring before 10 y ranges from 0% in the earliest cycles to essentially the whole cycle in the latest, and it is administrative, driven entirely by calendar time between the examination and the end of linkage. A single marginal Kaplan-Meier censoring distribution is therefore badly misspecified: it averages cycles whose censoring hazards have no overlap. All inverse-probability-of-censoring weights in this re-analysis are estimated **within cycle**, and the effect of that change is reported in `results/metrics_v2/SUMMARY.md`.

## 7. Consequence for the analysis

1. Multiple imputation is the primary analysis. Complete-case results are retained only as a sensitivity analysis and are labelled as such.
2. The imputation model is outcome-aware: it contains the event indicators and the cause-specific Nelson-Aalen cumulative hazards, not the follow-up time itself.
3. Cycle enters the imputation model, and censoring weights are estimated within cycle.
4. Every evaluation table reports the at-risk fraction at the horizon, because a stratum can be nominally large and still contribute almost no information at 15 y.
